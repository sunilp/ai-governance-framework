# RA-007 evidence: Prohibited-use screen (Step 0)

Placeholder in the example evidence pack for the [governance gate](../../../reference-implementations/README.md). In a real pack this file is the record itself, or a pointer to it in the records system with its document ID, version and SHA-256.

- **Record:** Step 0 screen against EU AI Act Art. 5 and the internal prohibited-use criteria.
- **Where in this example:** [risk assessment](../risk-assessment.md), [GenAI risk matrix](../../../framework/risk-classification/genai-risk-matrix.md)
- **Gap in this example:** The example risk assessment does not include the Step 0 screen. A real pack records each criterion answered, with a rationale and the screen date, before scoring. This control is non-waivable.
- **Control:** RA-007 in the [control register](../../../framework/governance-operations/control-register.md)
